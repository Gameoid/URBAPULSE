The Layout

Our physical model is mapped into three distinct functional sectors:

Zone A: The Heat Zone

Traditional high-density asphalt areas. This zone features our Red LED navigation triggers and monitoring sensors.

Zone B: The Blue Zone

The resilience sector featuring Cool Pavement, subsurface hydronic loops, and strategic tree placement.

Infrastructure Nodes

Includes Solar-Powered Bus Stands which act as our main IoT hubs, housing the ESP32 and Ultrasonic Misters.

Civic Pathways

Designed with High Albedo materials to reflect solar radiation, creating a cooler micro-climate for pedestrians.
