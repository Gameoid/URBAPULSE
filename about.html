The Stevenson Shielding

To maintain data integrity, sensors are recessed 1-inch under the solar panel array. This "Stevenson Shielding" logic prevents solar heat soak while allowing ambient airflow, ensuring that UrbaPulse measures true ambient temperature, not radiant solar energy.
