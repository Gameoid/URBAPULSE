How URBAPULSE Works

1. Dual-Controller Hardware

An Arduino Nano drives the 3-Road Smart LEDs (Main, School & Hut Roads), warning buzzer, and public routing LCD #1, while an ESP32 handles live DHT11 & MQ-6/135 telemetry, LCD #2, and Wi-Fi streaming.

2. High-Albedo & Solar Shielding

Reflective white road borders and solar-capped buildings bounce away solar heat while powering the node off-grid. Sensors sit 1 inch under the solar canopy (Stevenson Screen) for accurate shade readings.

3. Better Maps Health Routing

Calculates path weights using Distance + Temperature + AQI—guiding pedestrians away from 44°C corridors onto cooled green lanes, cutting heat exposure by 6°C and pollution by over 40%.

4. Built For Everyone

Created by Aditya Prakash, Hardik & Vaibhav (MVN Sector-17, Faridabad) so both smartphone users and pedestrians without phones stay safe on city streets.
